# Disclaimer

This repository contains an evolving **theoretical and academic thought experiment**.

It explores possible relationships between artificial intelligence, automation, economics, public services, democratic governance, sustainability, science and the long-term development of human civilization.

## What this work is

- a conceptual framework
- a philosophical and interdisciplinary exploration
- a structured set of hypotheses and design questions
- an invitation to research, criticism and debate

## What this work is not

It is **not**:

- a prediction of the future
- a claim of scientific consensus
- a government programme
- a political party platform
- legal, financial, medical or investment advice
- an operational plan for replacing existing institutions
- evidence that artificial general intelligence, superintelligence or machine consciousness will necessarily exist

Some sections intentionally explore speculative scenarios. These scenarios should not be interpreted as forecasts.

## Evidence and uncertainty

Factual claims should be supported by appropriate sources where possible. The manifesto also contains normative arguments, hypotheses and open questions that cannot be resolved solely through empirical evidence.

Future versions should increasingly separate:

- sourced empirical observations
- interpretations
- normative positions
- speculative scenarios

## AI assistance

AI systems may be used for drafting support, editing, translation, argument mapping, source discovery, consistency checking and exploration of counterarguments.

AI systems are not listed as authors.

**Albert Sanahuja Llopis retains conceptual ownership, editorial control and final responsibility for the published text.**

## Safety and ethics

The manifesto supports the development of AI as a beneficial tool for humanity and rejects harmful operational uses such as weapon development, biological threats, destructive cyberattacks or coercive systems.

Discussion of such risks is intended for prevention, governance and safety analysis.

## Living document

This is a versioned working document. Its conclusions may change as evidence improves, technology changes, critiques are incorporated, assumptions are revised and new scientific or social constraints become relevant.

Consult the repository version and `CHANGELOG.md` when citing the work.
