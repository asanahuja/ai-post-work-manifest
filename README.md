# AI-Assisted Post-Work Civilization Manifesto

A theoretical and academic thought experiment on how advanced artificial intelligence, automation, universal basic services, democratic governance, sustainability and scientific progress could reshape human civilization.

This repository is not presented as a prediction, a scientific consensus, a political programme, or a finished policy proposal. It is an evolving conceptual framework authored and approved by **Albert Sanahuja Llopis**, developed through critical reflection and AI-assisted drafting and structuring.

## Main themes

The manifesto explores, among other questions:

- AI as universal cognitive infrastructure
- Universal Basic Services
- post-work and reduced compulsory labour
- democratic and auditable AI governance
- distribution of AI-generated productivity
- public, common and cooperative infrastructure
- universal healthcare and access to knowledge
- planetary resource management and digital twins
- population and ecological sustainability
- abundant energy and long-term civilization planning
- space settlement and physical limits
- fundamental science and quantum physics
- AI safety, alignment and defence in depth
- governance without regulatory or corporate capture
- concentration of compute, data and cognitive power

## Academic and theoretical nature

This work is intentionally speculative and interdisciplinary. It combines philosophical, social, economic, technological and scientific questions.

Statements in the manifesto should be read according to their nature:

- **normative claims** express values or desired directions
- **empirical claims** should be supported by evidence when they are presented as factual
- **scenarios** are possibilities, not predictions
- **open questions** are deliberately unresolved
