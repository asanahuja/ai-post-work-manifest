# AGENTS.md

## Purpose

This repository contains an evolving **theoretical and academic thought experiment** about an AI-assisted post-work civilization.

AI agents may assist with research, editing, translation, consistency checks, source verification, counterargument analysis and repository maintenance. They are **not authors, owners or final decision-makers** for the manifesto.

## Authority and approval

**Albert Sanahuja Llopis is the author, copyright holder, repository owner and final editorial authority.**

No agent, automated workflow, contributor or external reviewer may treat a proposed change as an approved modification unless Albert Sanahuja Llopis has explicitly approved it.

The canonical text is:

- `MANIFEST.ca.md`

Authorized translations are:

- `MANIFEST.es.md`
- `MANIFEST.en.md`

If translations conflict with the Catalan source, the Catalan version prevails.

## Editorial principles

When modifying or reviewing the manifesto:

1. Preserve the distinction between normative proposals, empirical claims, hypotheses, scenarios and open questions.
2. Do not present speculative futures as established facts.
3. Do not silently replace the author's framing with a conventional political, economic or technological framework.
4. Do not force the text into a predefined ideology unless the author explicitly chooses that framing.
5. Preserve the manifesto's central concern with human dignity, universal access to basic needs, freedom and diversity, democratic governance, distribution of technological benefits, sustainability, scientific progress and AI safety.
6. Explicitly surface trade-offs, uncertainty, failure modes and counterarguments.
7. Avoid sensationalism about AI extinction, superintelligence, consciousness or loss of control.
8. For factual or time-sensitive claims, verify sources before presenting them as established facts.
9. Never add personal data, credentials, API keys or confidential information.
10. Keep the writing direct, technically serious and understandable.

### Versioning

The project follows **MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH**:

- **MAJOR**: major conceptual change or stable public edition
- **MINOR**: new sections or relevant conceptual additions
- **PATCH**: language, formatting, references or minor precision changes

### Approval rule

A change is official only after explicit approval by **Albert Sanahuja Llopis**.

Pull requests may contain proposals, but merging them into the canonical branch should be treated as editorial approval only when performed or explicitly approved by Albert.

## Translation workflow

When the canonical Catalan text changes:

1. update `MANIFEST.ca.md`
2. record the change in `CHANGELOG.md`
3. synchronize `MANIFEST.es.md`
4. synchronize `MANIFEST.en.md`
5. ensure headings, principles, qualifications and version metadata remain aligned
6. do not introduce concepts only in a translation

## Research workflow

For research-backed additions:

- prefer primary sources, peer-reviewed research, public institutions and original technical documentation
- record sources close to the claim they support
- distinguish evidence from interpretation
- note when evidence is preliminary, contested or scenario-dependent
- avoid using a single company, government or ideological source as the sole basis for broad social claims

## Safety

This repository discusses beneficial, safe and democratic uses of AI.

Do not add operational instructions for harmful activities such as:

- weapon construction
- biological weapon development
- destructive cyber operations
- sabotage of critical infrastructure
- evasion of safety systems
- coercive population control

High-level analysis of these risks is allowed when it supports governance, prevention, resilience or safety.
